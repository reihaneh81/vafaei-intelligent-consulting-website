About

Loyalty, Faithfulness, and Commitment, translated into how AI gets built.

Vafaei Intelligent Consulting is an AI transformation and solutions architecture consultancy that helps organizations identify, design, and implement practical AI, automation, and integration solutions that deliver measurable business value.

Back to home

The name Vafaei is derived from the Persian word Vafa, meaning loyalty, Faithfulness, and Commitment. These values are at the core of the company. Vafaei Intelligent Consulting is committed to being a trusted partner to its clients, building long-term relationships based on integrity, transparency, reliability, and a genuine dedication to their success.

Founded by Reihaneh Vafaei, an AI Solutions Architect and Transformation Consultant with over nine years of experience in technology, automation, integration, and digital transformation, the company combines deep technical expertise with a strong understanding of business processes.

The practice works closely with business leaders, product owners, and technical teams to discover opportunities where AI can create meaningful impact. Services span AI strategy and roadmap development, readiness assessments, solution architecture, intelligent automation, agentic AI, and enterprise integrations.

By combining business insight, architectural thinking, and hands-on technical expertise, Vafaei Intelligent Consulting helps organizations transform complex challenges into scalable, secure, and future-ready solutions, focused on empowering people and improving processes, not just adopting new technology.
